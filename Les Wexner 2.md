**Leslie Herbert Wexner**[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-3) (born September 8, 1937) is an American [billionaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billionaire "Billionaire") businessman and political activist. He is the co-founder and chair emeritus of [Bath & Body Works, Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bath_%26_Body_Works,_Inc. "Bath & Body Works, Inc.")[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-AoA-4) He has been the principal in [Abercrombie & Fitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abercrombie_%26_Fitch "Abercrombie & Fitch"), [Victoria's Secret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria%27s_Secret "Victoria's Secret") and [La Senza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Senza "La Senza"), amongst several other retail corporations.

Wexner is involved in politics. In 1991, with billionaire [Charles Bronfman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Bronfman "Charles Bronfman"), Wexner formed the [Mega Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Study_Group_\(Jewish_group\) "Study Group (Jewish group)"),[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Skolnik2-5) a club of some of the country's wealthiest and most influential businessmen. As a [pro-Israel lobby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_lobby_in_the_United_States "Israel lobby in the United States") group, the organization is also said to have tried to influence [US foreign policy in the Middle East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_foreign_policy_in_the_Middle_East "US foreign policy in the Middle East"). In 2003, it employed consultant [Frank Luntz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Luntz "Frank Luntz") to help the group mobilize support for [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel "Israel").[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-:0-6) The group is also said to have had contacts with the Israeli intelligence agency [Mossad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossad "Mossad") and to have served as a "vehicle for influence operations in the United States".[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-:2-7)

Wexner retained [Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein "Jeffrey Epstein") as his financial manager from 1987 to 2007 and was initially the "main client" of Epstein's money-management firm, according to [Bloomberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_News "Bloomberg News").[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-8) Epstein ran his business from a house Wexner owned and sometimes lived in.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Steel-9)[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-10) On February 10, 2026, Representative [Ro Khanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ro_Khanna "Ro Khanna") revealed that the FBI had named Wexner as an unindicted co-conspirator.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-11) The following day, the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Ohio denied Wexner's motion to quash a previous subpoena, ordering him to attend a deposition in an [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University "Ohio State University") case regarding abuse by former staff physician Richard Strauss, who was implicated in the [Ohio State University abuse scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University_abuse_scandal "Ohio State University abuse scandal").[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-10tv-12)

## Early life and education

Wexner was born in [Dayton, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton,_Ohio "Dayton, Ohio"),[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Hecht-13) on September 8, 1937, to parents Bella [née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name "Birth name") Cabakoff (1908–2001) and Harry Louis Wexner (1899–1975).[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Baumgold-14)[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Saxon-15) Both his parents were of Russian-Jewish origin.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-AoA-4) His father was born in [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia "Russia") and his mother was born in [Williamsburg, Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williamsburg,_Brooklyn "Williamsburg, Brooklyn").[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-AoA-4) She moved to [Columbus, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus,_Ohio "Columbus, Ohio"), as a toddler.[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Saxon-15) Wexner has a younger sister, Susan.[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Meyers-16)

Wexner attended [Bexley High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bexley_High_School "Bexley High School") and [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University "Ohio State University").[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Meyers-16) In 1953, he won a minor award for his essay, published in the _Ohio Jewish Chronicle_, titled "Why I Love and Respect Judaism".[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-17) He initially expressed an interest in architecture,[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Adler-18) but graduated in 1959[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Pyle-19) with a major in business administration.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Hecht-13) While a student at Ohio State University, he joined the [Sigma Alpha Mu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_Alpha_Mu "Sigma Alpha Mu") fraternity. Wexner served in the Air National Guard.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-AoA-4) He briefly attended the [Moritz College of Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University_Moritz_College_of_Law "Ohio State University Moritz College of Law").[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Meyers-16)

## Career

Wexner began his retail career working in his parents' clothing store, "Leslie's", which had been named after him.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Adler-18) According to Wexner, he began working at his parents' store so they could take a vacation.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Adler-18) Wexner analyzed the sales and inventory, identifying the most and least profitable items. When his father refused to adjust the inventory, Wexner decided to open his own store.[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Alexander-20)

In 1963, Wexner's aunt lent him $5,000, which he combined with a matching loan from a bank in order to start [The Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Limited "The Limited").[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Meyers-16)[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Adler-18) The store took its name from its focus on moderately priced merchandise, such as skirts, sweaters and shirts, that sold quickly and quickly generated revenue.[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Alexander-20) Wexner opened the first store on August 10, 1963, in the [Kingsdale Shopping Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingsdale_Shopping_Center "Kingsdale Shopping Center") in [Upper Arlington, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Arlington,_Ohio "Upper Arlington, Ohio"), a suburb of Columbus. One year later, Wexner's parents closed their store and joined their son in running The Limited.[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Saxon-15) He opened the second Limited store in August 1964.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Adler-18) He took Limited Brands public in 1969, listed as LTD on the [NYSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Stock_Exchange "New York Stock Exchange").[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Adler-18)

[A. Alfred Taubman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._Alfred_Taubman "A. Alfred Taubman") served as a mentor for Wexner, starting in the mid-1960s, and the two partnered on many deals involving Taubman's [shopping malls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopping_mall "Shopping mall").[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Meyers-16) In 1972, Robert H. Morosky, an active Catholic fund raiser, sold his house and moved into a small apartment in order to buy stock in The Limited and to serve as Vice Chairman of The Limited's board having full confidence and belief in Wexner.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Baumgold-14) Morosky resigned in 1987 and gave a number of interviews decades later criticizing Wexner's closeness with Jeffrey Epstein.[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-21)

Wexner expanded The Limited considerably in the 1970s, having opened the 100th store in 1976.[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Meyers-16) He took on significant debt in 1978 to purchase the importer Mast Industries, which provided him with essential business advantages over competitors.[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Meyers-16)[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Catholic_Press-22) In 1982, Wexner spent $105 million to purchase the much larger [Lane Bryant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lane_Bryant "Lane Bryant") retail chain of plus-sized clothing which came with $30 million in debt[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Catholic_Press-22) and acquired the [lingerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingerie "Lingerie") business [Victoria's Secret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria%27s_Secret "Victoria's Secret") from [Roy Raymond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roy_Raymond "Roy Raymond") for an undisclosed amount of stock, and $1 million[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Rushe-23)[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Catholic_Press-22) with Raymond later describing Wexner as "very guarded", stating, "When I met him, it was as if he met the devil."[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Adler-18) Six months later, when Raymond was facing bankruptcy, he contacted Wexner and offered to sell Victoria's Secret.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Adler-18) By 1992, Victoria's Secret was worth an estimated $1 billion,[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Rushe-23) and became known for the use of supermodels featured in an annual [fashion show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fashion_show "Fashion show"), overseen by [Ed Razek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Razek "Edward Razek").[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Rushe-23) In 1993, Wexner hired [Len Schlesinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Schlesinger "Leonard Schlesinger"), a Harvard Business School professor, whom he later appointed as a company director, to advise him.[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-24)[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-25)

Over the years, Wexner built L Brands, a retailing and marketing conglomerate that included Victoria's Secret, [Pink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_\(Victoria%27s_Secret\) "Pink (Victoria's Secret)") (Victoria's Secret for teens), Bath & Body Works, [Henri Bendel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Bendel "Henri Bendel"), The White Barn Candle Company, and [La Senza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Senza "La Senza"). Previous brands that were spun off include [Lane Bryant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lane_Bryant "Lane Bryant"), [Abercrombie & Fitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abercrombie_%26_Fitch "Abercrombie & Fitch"), [Lerner New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lerner_New_York "Lerner New York"), The Limited Too (now Tween Brands, Inc.), Structure 9, Aura Science, The Limited (which closed its brick-and-mortar stores while retaining its online presence), and Express (which closed its Canadian stores and hundreds of its U.S.-based stores). In 2012, _[CNN Money](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN_Money "CNN Money")_ described Wexner as the longest serving CEO of a [_Fortune_ 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortune_500 "Fortune 500") company.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-26) He was on Harvard Business Review's Top 100 Best Performing CEOs in the World, ranked number 11 in 2015,[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-27) and number 34 in 2016.[[28]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-28) In February 2020, Wexner announced that he was transitioning from CEO of L Brands into the role of chair emeritus.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-AoA-4)

### Corporate board memberships

Wexner was on the board of directors of [Banc One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banc_One "Banc One") from at least 1986 to 1991.[[29]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-29) Until 2002, he also sat on the Board of Directors of [Hollinger International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollinger_International "Hollinger International") together with [Henry Kissinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Kissinger "Henry Kissinger").[[30]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-30) He also served on the board of [Sotheby's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sotheby%27s "Sotheby's"), [American Ballet Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Ballet_Theatre "American Ballet Theatre") and the [Whitney Museum of American Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Museum_of_American_Arts "Whitney Museum of American Arts").[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Baumgold-14)

### Shapiro murder investigation

In 1985, Arthur L. Shapiro was a lawyer and partner at Columbus law firm Schwartz Shapiro Kelm & Warren,[[31]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-TCD-31) assigned to represent The Limited account.[[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-TDB-32) Shapiro was to appear before a grand jury to testify about an illegal tax scheme that he had been involved in.

Days before his testimony, Shapiro was assassinated in a way that witnesses described as similar to a Mafia hit. Because of a potential nexus between Shapiro, Mafia, and Wexner, as part of the police investigation, many people and entities connected to Wexner were looked at for any connections to the mob. This was detailed in a police report, later dubbed "The Arthur Shapiro Murder File", which found some tenuous connections between Wexner and the mob, in some of Wexner's businesses such as a trucking company. A local police chief said the report's theories were highly speculative and not based on hard evidence. The main suspect in Shapiro's murder remained Shapiro's business partner Berry Kessler – no connection to Wexner – who had a history of murdering his (Kessler's) business partners by contracting Mafia hit men. Kessler was involved with Shapiro in the illegal tax avoidance schemes, had a motive to silence him, had a history of knocking off business partners, and was seen giving someone a lot of cash the day after the murder, who matched witness descriptions of the killer. Kessler died in prison in 2005 for a different murder, and never admitted to the killing. The Shapiro murder was never officially solved.[[31]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-TCD-31)[[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-TDB-32)[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-33)

Wexner hired [Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein "Jeffrey Epstein") as his financial manager from 1987 to 2007.[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Thomas_Jr.-34)[[35]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-35) He was the primary client of Epstein, who claimed to only work with clients with a net worth of one billion USD or greater. Wexner purchased his New York property, the [Herbert N. Straus House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_N._Straus_House "Herbert N. Straus House"), in 1989 and sold it to Epstein in the mid-1990s following Wexner's marriage to Abigail.[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Thomas_Jr.-34) In July 1991, Wexner granted Epstein [power of attorney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_of_attorney "Power of attorney")[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Steel-9) and also instated him as a trustee on the board of the [Wexner Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wexner_Foundation "Wexner Foundation").[[36]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-36) In the 1990s, Les Wexner and Jeffrey Epstein were involved in relocating [Southern Air Transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Air_Transport "Southern Air Transport") (a [CIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Intelligence_Agency "Central Intelligence Agency") front organization with ties to the [Iran-Contra affair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Contra_affair "Iran–Contra affair") and alleged [CIA drug smuggling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA_drug_trafficking_allegations "CIA drug trafficking allegations")) from Miami to Columbus. Southern Air transported goods related to Wexner's businesses, but in 1996, Customs agents found a hidden shipment of cocaine on one of the planes. Southern Air was shut down in 1998 after Wexner had received federal aid for the relocation of the airline, just weeks before the [CIA Inspector General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA_Inspector_General "CIA Inspector General") released its official findings on [Contra cocaine trafficking allegations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA_involvement_in_Contra_cocaine_trafficking "CIA involvement in Contra cocaine trafficking").[[37]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-37)

Wexner has been accused of failing to take action when complaints were raised against Epstein, after executives of L Brands reported in the mid-1990s that Epstein was abusing his power and connection to Wexner by posing as a recruiter for Victoria's Secret models.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Steel-9) [Maria Farmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Farmer "Maria Farmer") contacted local and federal authorities about an assault she allegedly endured by Epstein and [Ghislaine Maxwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghislaine_Maxwell "Ghislaine Maxwell") while working as an artist-in-residence on Wexner's Ohio property in 1996. Within a year of Farmer's complaint, actress Alicia Arden filed a police report in Los Angeles detailing that Epstein had misrepresented himself as a recruiter for Victoria's Secret prior to another alleged assault.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Steel-9) In early 2006, Epstein was charged in Florida with "multiple counts of molestation and unlawful sexual activity with a minor."[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Steel-9) _[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "The New York Times")_ reported that 18 months after the charges were filed, Wexner cut his ties with Epstein.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Steel-9)

In July 2019, Wexner was included on a list of Epstein's "10 co-conspirators" within an [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI "FBI") email, alongside [Ghislaine Maxwell,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghislaine_Maxwell "Ghislaine Maxwell") [Darren Indyke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darren_Indyke "Darren Indyke"), [Richard Kahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Kahn_\(accountant\) "Richard Kahn (accountant)"), [Jean-Luc Brunel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Luc_Brunel "Jean-Luc Brunel"), and [Leslie Groff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesley_Groff "Lesley Groff"), though Wexner continued to deny involvement.[[38]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Filby_2025-38) In August 2019, following Epstein's second incarceration and prior to his death, Wexner addressed the Wexner Foundation, releasing a written statement that his former financial advisor, Jeffrey Epstein, had "misappropriated vast sums of money" from him and from his family.[[39]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-39) Wexner retained the services of [Debevoise & Plimpton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debevoise_%26_Plimpton "Debevoise & Plimpton") criminal defense attorney and former U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York [Mary Jo White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Jo_White "Mary Jo White").[[40]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-40)

L Brands shareholders filed a complaint in the [Court of Chancery of Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware_Court_of_Chancery "Delaware Court of Chancery") on January 14, 2021, stating that Wexner, among others, created an "entrenched culture of [misogyny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misogyny "Misogyny"), bullying, and harassment," and was aware of abuses being committed by Jeffrey Epstein, which breached Wexner's [fiduciary duty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiduciary_duty "Fiduciary duty") to the company and devalued the brand. The complaint also names Wexner's wife, current chair Sarah E. Nash, and former marketing officer Ed Razek, whose "widely known misconduct" was allegedly allowed at the company.[[41]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-41) On July 30, 2021, L Brands agreed to a $90 million settlement to resolve derivative lawsuits stemming from claims that combine Ohio and Delaware actions.[[42]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-42)

Following the passage of the 2025 [Epstein Files Transparency Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epstein_Files_Transparency_Act "Epstein Files Transparency Act"), Wexner faced additional public scrutiny.[[43]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-43)[[38]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Filby_2025-38) In January 2026, Wexner was subpoenaed by the [US House Oversight Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_House_Committee_on_Oversight_and_Government_Reform "United States House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform") of Congress to sit for a deposition.[[44]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-44)[[45]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-45) On February 10, 2026, U.S. Congressman [Ro Khanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ro_Khanna "Ro Khanna"), on the floor of the U.S. House of Representatives, read the names of six prominent men that he said he had seen in the unredacted versions of the Epstein files, whose names had been redacted in the versions of those files that were released to the public by the U.S. Department of Justice. (On the previous day, the Justice Department had allowed selected U.S. Congresspeople to view the files, in a closed room, without any recording device.) Congressman Khanna said Les Wexner is listed in the files as someone considered an Epstein co-conspirator by the FBI.[[46]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-46)[_[non-primary source needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:No_original_research#Primary,_secondary_and_tertiary_sources "Wikipedia:No original research")_]

### Ohio State University trustee, donor, and Strauss, MD scandal

Wexner served on the Board of Trustees of Ohio State University from 1988 to 1997. In December 2005, he was appointed to his second term and was elected chair in 2009. It was announced in June 2012 that his chairmanship would end eight years before his term expired.[[47]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-47) On May 11, 2004, Wexner received the [Woodrow Wilson Award for Corporate Citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson_Awards "Woodrow Wilson Awards") at a dinner in Columbus. The award was presented by the [Woodrow Wilson International Center for Scholars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson_International_Center_for_Scholars "Woodrow Wilson International Center for Scholars") of the [Smithsonian Institution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smithsonian_Institution "Smithsonian Institution") in [Washington, DC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_DC "Washington, DC"). [_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_] On February 16, 2011, Wexner pledged a donation of $100 million to Ohio State, to be allocated to the university's academic Medical Center and James Cancer Hospital and Solove Research Institute, with additional gifts to the Wexner Center for the Arts and other areas. This gift is the largest in the university's history.[[48]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-48)

Through the L Brands Foundation, Wexner and L Brands contributed $163.4 million to the Columbus Foundation.[[49]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-49) On February 10, 2012, the Ohio State University board of trustees voted to rename the Ohio State University Medical Center to the [Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University_Wexner_Medical_Center "Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center"), commemorating "Mr. Wexner's indelible, lifelong legacy of leadership at Ohio State," according to university president E. Gordon Gee, during over 30 years of "ardent support" of the institution.[[50]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-50)[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Pyle-19)

Wexner faced additional public scrutiny in late 2019 and early 2020, when a group of wrestlers who are survivors of the [Ohio State University abuse scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University_abuse_scandal "Ohio State University abuse scandal") publicly called on state and federal officials to conduct further inquiry into [Maria Farmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Farmer "Maria Farmer")'s allegations of sexual assault at the Wexner property.[[51]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-51)[[52]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Siemaszko-52) The wrestlers called for accountability for the Wexner family's alleged involvement in Epstein's abuse and raised the issue of the continuing influence of Abigail and Leslie Wexner serving as the "biggest and best-known benefactors" of the university.[[52]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Siemaszko-52)

Fallout from the scandal continued into the 2020s with particular focus paid to the part played by Wexner and his association with Epstein. Former wrestlers and their supporters criticized the university's board for failing to look into Wexner and in their opinion covering up the matter. Wexner's legal team had been stalling efforts by victims to subpoena Wexner. Former wrestlers and their supporters noted that board chair John Zeiger was a close friend of Wexner and that his daughter and law partner was Wexner's attorney. Zeiger did not recuse himself from matters regarding Wexner.[[53]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-53)[[54]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-54)[[55]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-55) On February 11, 2026, [U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._District_Court_for_the_Southern_District_of_Ohio "U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Ohio") Judge [Michael H. Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_H._Watson "Michael H. Watson") denied Wexner's January motion to quash a previous subpoena, ordering him to attend a long-avoided deposition in a case regarding abuses by former Ohio State University staff physician Richard Strauss, who was implicated in the [Ohio State University abuse scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University_abuse_scandal "Ohio State University abuse scandal"), before committing suicide in 2005.[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-10tv-12)

## Philanthropy

In 1989, Wexner and his mother Bella were the first to make a $1 million personal donation to the [United Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Way_of_America "United Way of America"). Both of their names were inscribed in marble and are on display in the lobby of the United Way Headquarters in [Alexandria, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria,_Virginia "Alexandria, Virginia").[[60]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-60) Wexner was listed by _Forbes_ in 2017, the wealthiest of seven billionaires from Ohio who made the list.[[61]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-61) He was a major funder of the [Wexner Center for the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wexner_Center_for_the_Arts "Wexner Center for the Arts") at the Ohio State University, which is named in honor of his father.[[62]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-62) Wexner said that because "growing up, my folks moved around a lot, and I never got a good Jewish education", he felt unprepared to take leadership roles in his Orthodox Jewish community.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Baumgold-14)[[63]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Wexner_Foundation-63) So, in 1985, he joined [Rabbi Herbert A. Friedman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbi_Herbert_A._Friedman "Rabbi Herbert A. Friedman") to establish the Wexner Foundation's first core program, aimed "to educate Jewish communal leaders in the history, thought, traditions, and contemporary challenges of the Jewish people."[[63]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Wexner_Foundation-63)

### Study group

In 1991, with billionaire [Charles Bronfman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Bronfman "Charles Bronfman"), Wexner formed the [Study Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Study_Group_\(Jewish_group\) "Study Group (Jewish group)"), more widely known as the Mega Group,[[64]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Skolnik-64) a loosely organized club of some of the country's wealthiest and most influential businessmen who were concerned with Jewish issues. [Max Fischer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Fisher "Max Fisher"), [Michael Steinhardt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Steinhardt "Michael Steinhardt"), [Leonard Abramson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Abramson "Leonard Abramson"), [Edgar Bronfman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_Bronfman_Sr. "Edgar Bronfman Sr."), and [Laurence Tisch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Tisch "Laurence Tisch") were some of the members. The group would meet twice a year for two days of seminars related to the topic of philanthropy and [Judaism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism "Judaism"). As a [pro-Israel lobby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_lobby_in_the_United_States "Israel lobby in the United States") group, the organization is also said to have tried to influence [US foreign policy in the Middle East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_foreign_policy_in_the_Middle_East "US foreign policy in the Middle East"). In 2003, it employed [Republican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_\(United_States\) "Republican Party (United States)") political consultant [Frank Luntz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Luntz "Frank Luntz") to help the group mobilize support for Israel.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-:0-6) Under the name MEGA, the group is also said to have had contacts with the Israeli intelligence agency [Mossad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossad "Mossad") and to have served as a "vehicle for influence operations in the United States".[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-:2-7)

In an April 1998 group meeting, [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg "Steven Spielberg") spoke about his personal religious journey.[[65]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-65) The Study Group, which Wexner co-chaired with Charles Bronfman, went on to inspire a number of philanthropic initiatives such as the [Partnership for Excellence in Jewish Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEJE "PEJE"), [Birthright Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birthright_Israel "Birthright Israel"), and the upgrading of [national Hillel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillel_International "Hillel International").[[64]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Skolnik-64)

## Personal life

In a 1985 article, Wexner discussed that he had trouble sleeping which he attributed to his [dybbuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dybbuk "Dybbuk") spirit that he dubbed "terminal _shpilkes_" (_shpilkes_ being Yiddish for "pin", as in "on pins and needles"), "the demon that always wakes up in the morning with Wexner and tweaks and pulls at him".[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Baumgold-14) He noted it had been intermittently appearing since childhood, and attributed his driving, his bachelor lifestyle and his business drive to trying to escape the anxiety it caused, always having music playing as he could not stand the sound of silence.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Baumgold-14) One girlfriend converted to Judaism and changed her name to "Cohen" after a year of dating Wexner.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-Baumgold-14) On January 23, 1993, Wexner married Abigail S. Koppel, an attorney.[[66]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-66) The couple has four children.[[67]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-67)

Formerly of the [Bexley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bexley,_Ohio "Bexley, Ohio") area, Wexner now lives in New Albany, a community northeast of Columbus. He owns a 30-room, $47 million, [Georgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_architecture "Georgian architecture")-inspired estate, on nearly 336 acres (1.36 km2), that was built in 1990. The estate was, for 20 years, the location of the Annual New Albany Classic Invitational Grand Prix & Family Day (an equestrian show) benefiting The Center for Family Safety and Healing. In February 2018, Abigail Wexner announced the end of the event, citing the growing number of equestrian competitions.[[68]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-68) Wexner has owned the mid-18th century [Foxcote House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxcote_House "Foxcote House") in Warwickshire, England, since 1997.[[69]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-69) Wexner was inducted as an honorary member of the 104th Sphinx Senior Class at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University "Ohio State University") on May 7, 2010.[[70]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-70)

## Political activities

[George W. Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Bush "George W. Bush") appointed Wexner to serve in the Honorary Delegation to accompany him to [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem "Jerusalem") for the celebration of the 60th anniversary of the [State of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Israel "State of Israel") in May 2008.[[71]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-71)[[72]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-72) Wexner hosted a fundraiser in 2012 for [Mitt Romney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitt_Romney "Mitt Romney"),[[73]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-73) and also donated $250,000 to [Restore Our Future](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restore_Our_Future "Restore Our Future"), Romney's [super PAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_PAC "Super PAC").[[74]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-74) In 2015, Wexner donated $500,000 to the Right to Rise USA super PAC that supported the [2016 presidential campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeb_Bush_presidential_campaign,_2016 "Jeb Bush presidential campaign, 2016") of [Jeb Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeb_Bush "Jeb Bush").[[75]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-75) _[The Columbus Dispatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Columbus_Dispatch "The Columbus Dispatch")_ reported on September 14, 2018, that Wexner had renounced his affiliation with the Republican Party, which he had been supporting since his college days, due to changes in its nature.[[76]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-76)[[77]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Wexner#cite_note-77)
