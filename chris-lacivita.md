# **Political Profile: Chris LaCivita Controversies**

**Chris LaCivita** is a veteran Republican political strategist who served as co-campaign manager for Donald Trump’s 2024 presidential campaign alongside Susie Wiles. Over his multi-decade career in political operations, he has been involved in several high-profile national controversies.

## **Key Controversies & Media Scrutiny**

### **1\. Foreign Consulting Work (Republika Srpska)**

Reports surfaced regarding LaCivita's international consulting arrangements involving foreign entities during the election cycle.

\[\!WARNING\]

**Key Concern: Pro-Russian Political Alignment**

* **Details:** LaCivita and fellow operative James Blair received hundreds of thousands of dollars to advise political leadership in **Republika Srpska** (the Serb-majority entity within Bosnia and Herzegovina), led by pro-Russian figures.  
* **Response:** When questioned about the arrangement, Donald Trump stated he would fire staff if outside consulting conflicted with campaign interests, emphasizing that the work was conducted through external consulting entities rather than direct campaign employment.

### **2\. The 2004 "Swift Boat Veterans for Truth" Campaign**

During the 2004 U.S. Presidential Election between George W. Bush and John Kerry, LaCivita served as the chief strategist for the group **Swift Boat Veterans for Truth**.

\[\!IMPORTANT\]

**Legacy: The Origin of "Swiftboating"**

* **Strategy:** The group launched a series of targeted television advertisements questioning Democratic nominee John Kerry's Vietnam War military record and decorations.  
* **Fact-Checks:** The claims were widely discredited by official U.S. Navy records, military service logs, fellow crew members, and independent news organizations.  
* **Political Impact:** The operation was so influential that the term **"swiftboating"** entered the political lexicon to describe aggressive, deceptive political smear tactics designed to undermine an opponent's key strength.

### **3\. Campaign Compensation & *The Daily Beast* Lawsuit**

Financial disclosures and media coverage during the 2024 presidential cycle brought significant scrutiny to campaign vendor payout structures.

\[\!NOTE\]

**Financial Scrutiny & Legal Dispute**

* **Vendor Payments:** Investigative reports detailed over \$22 million in campaign funds routed through consulting entities connected to LaCivita.  
* **Defamation Suit:** Following an article published by *The Daily Beast* detailing vendor pay structures, LaCivita filed a high-profile defamation lawsuit against the outlet.  
* **Outcome:** LaCivita later voluntarily withdrew and dismissed the lawsuit.

### **4\. Arlington National Cemetery Incident**

An August 2024 campaign visit by Donald Trump to Arlington National Cemetery resulted in a confrontation with staff enforcing federal guidelines.

\[\!CAUTION\]

**Clash with Military Authorities**

* **The Incident:** An altercation occurred when an Arlington employee attempted to enforce federal law prohibiting political photography and campaign activities in Section 60\.  
* **LaCivita's Response:** LaCivita publicly attacked the cemetery official, referring to the employee as a "despicable individual" and alleging they were suffering from a mental health crisis.  
* **Official Rebuke:** The U.S. Army issued a rare public statement rebuking the campaign and defending the employee, drawing criticism from military veterans and ethics watchdog groups.
