**Ayman Mohammed Rabie al-Zawahiri** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language "Arabic language"): أيمن محمد ربيع الظواهري, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic "Romanization of Arabic"): *ʾAyman Muḥammad Rabīʿ aẓ-Ẓawāhirī*;<sup><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-2"><span>[</span>a<span>]</span></a></sup> 19 June 1951 – 31 July 2022) was an Egyptian-born [pan-Islamist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan-Islamism "Pan-Islamism") militant and physician who served as the second general [emir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir "Emir") of [al-Qaeda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qaeda "Al-Qaeda") from June 2011 until [his death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Ayman_al-Zawahiri "Killing of Ayman al-Zawahiri") in July 2022. He is best known for being one of the main orchestrators of the [September 11 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/September_11_attacks "September 11 attacks").<sup><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-3"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup>

Al-Zawahiri graduated from [Cairo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo_University "Cairo University") with a degree in medicine and a master's degree in surgery and was a surgeon by profession. He became a leading figure in the [Egyptian Islamic Jihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_Islamic_Jihad "Egyptian Islamic Jihad"), an Egyptian [Islamist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamism "Islamism") organization, and eventually attained the rank of emir. He was imprisoned from 1981 to 1984 for his role in the [assassination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Anwar_Sadat "Assassination of Anwar Sadat") of Egyptian president [Anwar Sadat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anwar_Sadat "Anwar Sadat"). His actions against the [Egyptian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_government "Egyptian government"), including his planning of the 1995 [attack on the Egyptian Embassy in Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_on_the_Egyptian_Embassy_in_Pakistan "Attack on the Egyptian Embassy in Pakistan"), resulted in him being sentenced to death *[in absentia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trial_in_absentia "Trial in absentia")* during the 1999 " [Returnees from Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Returnees_from_Albania "Returnees from Albania") " trial.

A close associate of al-Qaeda leader [Osama bin Laden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osama_bin_Laden "Osama bin Laden"), al-Zawahiri held significant sway over the group's operations. He was wanted by the United States and the United Nations, respectively, for his role in the [1998 U.S. embassy bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_United_States_embassy_bombings "1998 United States embassy bombings") in Kenya and Tanzania and in the [2002 Bali bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Bali_bombings "2002 Bali bombings"). He merged the Egyptian Islamic Jihad with al-Qaeda in 2001 and formally became bin Laden's deputy in 2004. He succeeded bin Laden as al-Qaeda's leader after [bin Laden's death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Osama_bin_Laden "Killing of Osama bin Laden") in 2011. In May 2011, the U.S. announced a $25 million bounty for information leading to his capture.

On July 31, 2022, al-Zawahiri was [killed in a CIA drone strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Ayman_al-Zawahiri "Killing of Ayman al-Zawahiri") in Afghanistan.
