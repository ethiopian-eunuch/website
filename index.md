# **Trump Family & Extended Relatives in Public & Legal Controversies**

Over the years, several members of Donald Trump’s immediate family, extended relatives, and family by marriage have drawn public focus, legal scrutiny, or media controversy.

## **1\. Relatives by Marriage & In-Laws**
>[!WARNING]**Status: Employed as of 2026-10-08
>Location: Europe
>Note: Convicted criminal
### **Charles Kushner *(Father-in-Law to Ivanka Trump)***

* **Context:** Prominent New Jersey real estate developer.  
* **Key Issues:**  
  * Convicted in 2004 for tax evasion, illegal campaign contributions, and witness tampering (a scheme involving a secretly recorded encounter to pressure his brother-in-law).  
  * Served 14 months in federal prison.  
  * Received a full presidential pardon from Donald Trump in December 2020\.  
  * Nominated in late 2024 to serve as U.S. Ambassador to France.

### **Jared Kushner *(Son-in-Law)***

* **Context:** Senior Adviser to the President during the Trump administration.  
* **Key Issues:**  
  * High-profile scrutiny regarding his security clearance application and official communications.  
  * Post-white house scrutiny surrounding his private equity firm, **Affinity Partners**, which received a **\$2 billion investment** from Saudi Arabia's Sovereign Wealth Fund shortly after he left office.

## **2\. Immediate & Extended Family**

### **Donald Trump Jr. & Eric Trump *(Sons)***

* **Context:** Executive Vice Presidents of the Trump Organization.  
* **Key Issues:**  
  * **Donald Trump Jr.:** Drew major scrutiny for arranging the June 2016 Trump Tower meeting with a Russian attorney offering information on Hillary Clinton.  
  * **Both:** Co-defendants in the New York civil fraud lawsuit brought by the NY Attorney General concerning inflated asset valuations.

### **Mary L. Trump *(Niece)***

* **Context:** Daughter of Donald Trump’s late older brother, Fred Trump Jr., and a clinical psychologist.  
* **Key Issues:**  
  * Published a 2020 tell-all memoir, *Too Much and Never Enough*, offering a critical look at the family's internal dynamics and history.  
  * Engaged in high-profile legal battles with the family over non-disclosure agreements and estate settlements.

### **Fred Trump III *(Nephew)***

* **Context:** Son of Fred Trump Jr.  
* **Key Issues:**  
  * Published a memoir in 2024 titled *All in the Family: The Trumps and How We Got to Be This Way*.  
  * Made public allegations regarding Donald Trump's private comments toward individuals with severe disabilities (allegations denied by the Trump campaign).
