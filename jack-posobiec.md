⁠jack_posobiec⁠ — profile of political operative, digital commentator, and viral disinformation activist

DESCRIPTION
⁠jack_posobiec⁠ documents the public record, media tenure, military history, and viral disinformation campaigns associated with John "Jack" Michael Posobiec III (born December 14, 1984).
Posobiec is a far-right political activist, internet personality, and media commentator known for utilizing social media platforms to conduct information warfare, amplify conspiracy theories, and mobilize MAGA-aligned political movements.
OPTIONS
Outlets
 ⁠--outlet=rebel_media⁠
Briefly served as D.C. bureau chief for Canadian far-right outlet Rebel Media in 2017, obtaining temporary White House press credentials before being terminated over plagiarism allegations and uncredited material.
 ⁠--outlet=oann⁠
Served as a correspondent and host for One America News Network (OANN) from 2018 to 2021, broadcasting political commentary and interviewing far-right figures.
 ⁠--outlet=real_americas_voice⁠
Host of Human Events Daily on Real America's Voice network and Senior Editor at Human Events, serving as a platform for populist conservative activism.
Operations & Campaigns
 ⁠--operation=pizzagate⁠
Promoted the debunked "Pizzagate" hoax in 2016, claiming senior Democratic Party officials were involved in a human trafficking ring operating out of a D.C. pizzeria, which later resulted in an armed gunman firing a rifle inside the venue.
 ⁠--operation=macronleaks⁠
Coined and amplified the ⁠#MacronLeaks⁠ hashtag on Twitter two days prior to the 2017 French presidential election, disseminating stolen and altered emails from Emmanuel Macron's campaign.
 ⁠--operation=stop_the_steal⁠
Promoted disproven claims of voter fraud regarding the 2020 U.S. presidential election and participated in "Stop the Steal" rallies leading up to the January 6 Capitol attack.
MILITARY & INTELLIGENCE SERVICE
 U.S. Navy Reserve: Joined the Navy Reserve in 2010 as an intelligence specialist, commissioned as an officer in 2013, and performed human intelligence analysis at Joint Task Force Guantanamo Bay.
 Office of Naval Intelligence (ONI): Worked as a civilian intelligence analyst at ONI. His security clearance was suspended in 2017 following public scrutiny over his online political activism and disinformation activities, leading to his departure from military intelligence.
CONTROVERSIES & DISINFORMATION
 "Rape Melania" Sign Hoax (2016): Accused of organizing a false-flag operation during a post-election anti-Trump protest in D.C. by holding a sign reading "Rape Melania" to discredit anti-Trump demonstrators.
 Seth Rich Conspiracy: Signal-boosted false claims alleging DNC staffer Seth Rich was assassinated for leaking internal emails to WikiLeaks.
 Extremist Connections: Repeatedly highlighted by civil rights watchdog organizations (e.g., SPLC, ADL) for interacting with, boosting, or citing white nationalists, neo-Nazis, and far-right extremist figures on social media.
BUGS AND KNOWN ISSUES
 Verification Failures: Content repeatedly flagged by fact-checking organizations and social media platforms for containing fabricated screenshots, unvetted rumors, and state-backed intelligence leaks.
 Plagiarism: Terminated from Rebel Media in May 2017 after copying script material without attribution from far-right activist Jason Kessler.
