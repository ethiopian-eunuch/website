# Hillary Clinton deposition on Epstein and Maxwell investigation

## Summary

The House Oversight Committee released a transcript from a closed-door deposition involving former Secretary of State Hillary Clinton as part of its review of the federal government’s handling of the Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell investigations. In the testimony, Clinton said she did not recall ever meeting Epstein, said she had no knowledge of his crimes before they became public, and argued the committee was broadening the scope beyond what was relevant to the federal investigation.

This file combines a concise summary with the hearing transcript for readability and archival use.

## Date
March 2, 2026

## Sources
- WJAC News
- House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform
- Public deposition transcript excerpts

## Key points

- Clinton said she did not recall ever meeting Jeffrey Epstein.
- She said she was not aware of Epstein’s criminal activities before they became public.
- She said she had no direct knowledge that would assist the committee’s investigation.
- She criticized the hearing as politically motivated and said it was not focused on the real federal enforcement issues.
- She argued the committee should focus on law enforcement records, accountability, and those with direct knowledge of the crimes.
